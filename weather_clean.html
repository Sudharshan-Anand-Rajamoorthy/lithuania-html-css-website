Seasons Overview

Winter

Winter in Lithuania typically lasts from December to February. Temperatures often drop below freezing, and snowfall is common. Lakes and rivers may freeze, creating picturesque landscapes perfect for winter activities.

Spring

Spring begins in March and lasts through May. The weather gradually warms, and nature awakens with blooming flowers and budding trees. Rain showers are frequent, helping to nourish the countryside.

Summer

Summer spans from June to August and is characterized by warm temperatures and long daylight hours. It is the best time for outdoor activities, with average temperatures ranging from 15°C to 25°C.

Autumn

Autumn, from September to November, brings cooler temperatures and vibrant foliage. It is a transitional season leading into winter. Foggy mornings and shortening daylight are common.

Seasonal Highlights

Each season in Lithuania offers unique experiences:

  • Winter: Snow-covered landscapes and ice skating on frozen lakes.
  • Spring: Blooming nature and refreshing rain showers.
  • Summer: Warm days perfect for beach visits and festivals.
  • Autumn: Colorful foliage and cozy evenings.

Lithuania experiences all four seasons distinctly, each offering unique weather patterns and natural beauty.

The quality of weather in Lithuania is generally favorable and contributes positively to daily life and seasonal activities. Air quality remains high throughout most of the year, especially in the greener regions and along the coast.
